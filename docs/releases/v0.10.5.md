# ObsiRead 0.10.5

- **Listening saves your place.** Stop listening, and the article opens there next time. Up next starts it there too.
- **Next goes to the next article.** On the last sentence, Next starts the next article in Up next.
- **The end is clear.** When Up next is done, Listening now goes away and Play all comes back.
- **The right count.** The number on the Up next tab no longer counts the article playing now.
- **No stuck queue.** An article that can no longer open leaves Up next, so the rest still plays.
