# ObsiRead 0.10.4

- **Up next plays.** Articles in Up next are read aloud one after another. When one ends, it leaves the list and the next one starts where you stopped reading it.
- **Play from the list.** Tap Play all, or the play button on any article.
- **The player follows you.** The small player now shows above the tabs too, so you can pause or change speed from anywhere.
