# ObsiRead 0.3.0

- New app icon: an obsidian stone and a book, purple on black.
- Comments on every site load in full. The app taps "more comments" and "more replies" for you.
- Comment threads read cleanly. Each reply sits under its parent with a thin line. Names have their own colour.
- Comments go to Obsidian as nested quotes, like the Web Clipper writes them.
- Hacker News comments keep their author names.
- The link card no longer goes under the top bar.
- Articles with no picture show a tile with the site's first letter.
- Small fixes: no empty "..." headings, cleaner text in Obsidian.
