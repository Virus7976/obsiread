The first ObsiRead.

- Share a web page from your browser. ObsiRead reads it like Obsidian Web Clipper and shows it in a clean reader.
- Pictures from the article show in place. Nothing extra is added.
- Highlight, add notes, and clip to your Obsidian vault with your templates.
- Contents: tap the time left at the bottom to see every heading. The one you are in is marked.
- Links open in a reading card inside the app. Tap Save to keep the page.
- A page that wants you to sign in opens in the app's browser. Sign in, then tap Read this page.
