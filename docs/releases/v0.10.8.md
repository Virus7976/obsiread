# ObsiRead 0.10.8

- **Save audio from the article menu.** Tap ⋮ on an article, then Save audio for offline. The list shows "Saving audio" while it works and "Audio saved" when it is done.
- **Saved articles open faster.** A long page is now laid out once, with its pictures in, instead of again for each picture.
- **Listen starts where you are reading.** Scroll to a new place and tap Listen. It reads from there, not from where it last stopped.
