# ObsiRead 0.10.1

- **One voice list.** The player lists Microsoft Edge voices first, then your phone's voices and Google's.
- **Fewer buttons.** Player options now open in menus, like Apple Music. Nothing sits at the bottom of the sheet.
- **Smooth player.** The small player now glides down with the bars when they hide.
- **Templater templates keep new properties.** A property you add in the app, like tags, is now written into the template in Obsidian and stays there.
- **Property names from your vault.** When you add a property, start to type. The app shows the names your vault already uses, so a typo never makes a second property.
- **Saved audio count.** Settings shows the right number of saved articles.
