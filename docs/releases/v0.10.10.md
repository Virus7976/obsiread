# ObsiRead 0.10.10

- **Hide pictures.** Tap ⋮ in an article, then Show pictures, to turn all pictures off. Use it for pages you would rather not see. Tap it again to show them. The page stays at the line you were reading.
- **The player notification shows the whole article.** The time bar is for the full article now, like other audio apps. Drag it to jump.
