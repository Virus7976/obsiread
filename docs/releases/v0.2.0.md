Reading controls.

- Tap the page to show or hide the bars. While you read, only the text is left, with a small % in the corner.
- The bars float over the text, so it never jumps. Scrolling up works smoothly again.
- New Aa button: a Reading sheet with four tabs.
  - Text: five fonts, size and weight.
  - Layout: line spacing, paragraph gap, letter spacing, side margins, line length, justify and hyphenation.
  - Theme: Paper, Sepia, Gray, Night, Contrast, Sunset, Grass, Nord and Solarized, or follow the app.
  - Behavior: tap to show the bars, hide them as you read, full screen, progress, links in the app.
- The sheet does not dim the page, so you see each change as you make it.
- Fixed: lines squashed together after Read the page again.
