# ObsiRead 0.10.2

- **Sync.** Scroll away while an article is read to you, and a Sync button shows on the player. Tap it to go back to the spoken word.
- **How far you are.** The player shows the percent read, next to the minutes left.
- **Progress notices.** Saving audio and downloading an update now show their progress in a quiet notification. It says when the work is done, or why it stopped.
