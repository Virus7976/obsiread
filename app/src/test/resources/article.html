Why Slow Reading Wins

By Ada Lovelace

Most of us read too fast. We skim a page, jump to the end, and forget it all by dinner. This essay is about a better way, one that takes a little longer and gives much more back.

When you read slowly, you give each idea room. You notice how one sentence leans on the next. The words stop being a blur and start being a voice. One study found that slow readers remembered twice as much a week later.

How to start

Start with something short. Here is a simple plan you can try tonight, with nothing more than a book and a pencil:

  • Pick one essay, not a whole book.
  • Read it out loud, at the pace of speech.
  • Write one line about it when you finish.

Reading is to the mind what exercise is to the body.

Some people like to keep notes in code, like notes.md, so they can search them later:

grep -i "slow" notes.md
wc -l notes.md

Try it for one week. You will be surprised how much stays with you.
